Frequently Asked Questions

Go to resova.com/login and click "Forgot Password." Use your venue manager email address. If you still cannot access your account after the reset, submit a support ticket below with the category "Booking System" and we will escalate to Resova directly. Typical resolution time is under 2 hours during business hours.
First, check the game-specific troubleshooting guide on the Games page. Most prop issues have a quick fix documented there. If the issue requires a replacement part, submit a maintenance ticket below with photos of the damage. Include the game name, room number, and prop description. HQ will ship replacement parts within 3-5 business days for stocked items.
All brand-approved marketing assets are available on the Marketing page. This includes logos (all formats), game photography, social media templates, print-ready files, and the complete brand guidelines. For custom creative requests — such as local event flyers or co-branded materials — submit a marketing request through the form on that page. Allow 5-7 business days for custom work.
Start by power-cycling the terminal (unplug for 30 seconds, then reconnect). Verify your internet connection is stable and that the terminal is on the correct Wi-Fi network. If the issue persists, check for any pending software updates on the device. For hardware failures, submit an urgent support ticket below. We maintain spare terminals at HQ and can overnight a replacement if needed.
Follow the Onboarding Checklist under Pillar 01 on the Operations page. You will need to: (1) submit their info for Resova user creation, (2) enroll them in Escapology University LMS, (3) set up their scheduling account, and (4) begin the GM Certification Program. The full onboarding process takes approximately 14 days. If you need system access set up urgently, submit a ticket with the "HR/Staffing" category.
For true emergencies (water leak, power outage, safety hazard), contact your regional facilities manager directly — see the HQ Ops Contacts directory. For non-emergency maintenance requests, submit a ticket below with priority "High" and the "Maintenance/Facilities" category. Include photos and a description of the issue's impact on operations. Most non-emergency requests are scheduled within 48 hours.
Wi-Fi credentials for your venue are stored in your venue's secure operations folder on the shared drive. For new workstation setup (POS terminal, game control station, or office computer), submit a ticket with the "IT/Technical" category. Include the device type, intended use, and any software that needs to be installed. IT will provide remote setup instructions or schedule a configuration call within 24 hours.
